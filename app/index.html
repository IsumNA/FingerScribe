About

Your handwriting is read on this device, instantly. NVIDIA Nemotron (on Nebius Token Factory) then fixes misread words using the sentence around them, finishes words early, links each note to related earlier ones, draws your mind map, tidies notes and transcribes voice notes.

Word locking

Instant: a word is fixed the moment you start the next one (fastest, may lock a misread). Careful: waits until two readings agree (a little slower, more accurate).

Teach your pen

The more of your own writing it has seen, the better it reads you. Write what it shows in your normal style, then tap Save: the recording goes to your Downloads folder. Sentences help most with fast, joined writing (30 sentences, about 5 minutes).
